🔗 SMA Crossover (Golden Cross / Death Cross)

Simple Moving Average (SMA) crossover is a trend-following strategy. A "Golden Cross" occurs when a short-term SMA (e.g., 50-day) crosses above a long-term SMA (e.g., 200-day), signaling a bullish trend. Conversely, a "Death Cross" signals a bearish trend.

📈 RSI Breakout (Relative Strength Index)

RSI is a momentum oscillator that measures the speed and change of price movements. Traditionally, an RSI above 70 indicates an overbought condition (potential sell), while below 30 indicates an oversold condition (potential buy).

Read more on Investopedia →

📊 Bollinger Bands

These consist of a middle SMA with two outer bands that represent standard deviations of the price. They help identify volatility and potential overextended price levels. When price touches the upper band, it may be overbought; the lower band may indicate oversold.

Read more on Investopedia →

📉 MACD (Moving Average Convergence Divergence)

MACD is a trend-following momentum indicator that shows the relationship between two moving averages of a security’s price. It's used to trigger buy and sell signals through crossovers and divergence.
